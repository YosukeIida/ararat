# Ararat and Yab UI values

Yab source revision `5a9b57a94cdfbc9bb181b8bab26c1f0611ddf245`. Ararat baseline `3e7509f`. Section numbers refer to [Ararat’s architecture](../architecture.md). The Cra column uses [the Cra ledger](cra-ledger.md), the values attributed in §7, and the local Cra source at `68108cea7a2f8759c1da7e7756abf446f6c76efe`. Earlier Yab screen readings come from [the observation log](yab-observed.md). Source timings and historical screen measurements can differ.

“Yes” means the value serves the same interaction. “Partial” means Yab implements a related interaction. “No” means Yab uses a different mechanism or has no counterpart for the item. “Unconfirmed” marks a counterpart or a source value that could not be established. A dash means no Cra value was established.

## Differences from Arc measurements

Ararat’s floating sidebar starts after 150 ms within 5 pt of the window’s edge, uses a 150 ms response and 0.84 damping, and leaves in about 90 ms. It keeps the pointer up to 250 pt outside the leading edge and 72 pt past the panel on the other side. Yab uses a 300 ms dwell and a 3 pt inner edge region, with no fixed outside distance on the same display. Its panel spring uses a 210 ms response and 0.78 damping, the exit lasts 120 ms, and its floating inset is 0 pt against Ararat’s 5 pt. The older Yab observation found a 12 pt trigger, roughly 330 ms dwell and an estimated 170 ms response. Use the committed values when comparing implementations. [Arc measurements](arc-ardia-observed.md), [earlier Yab measurements](yab-observed.md), [Lights.swift:648-685](https://github.com/yetanotherbrowser/yab/blob/5a9b57a94cdfbc9bb181b8bab26c1f0611ddf245/Sources/Fast/Lights.swift#L648-L685), [Lights.swift:687-727](https://github.com/yetanotherbrowser/yab/blob/5a9b57a94cdfbc9bb181b8bab26c1f0611ddf245/Sources/Fast/Lights.swift#L687-L727), [Peek.swift:848-854](https://github.com/yetanotherbrowser/yab/blob/5a9b57a94cdfbc9bb181b8bab26c1f0611ddf245/Sources/Fast/Peek.swift#L848-L854), [Design.swift:548](https://github.com/yetanotherbrowser/yab/blob/5a9b57a94cdfbc9bb181b8bab26c1f0611ddf245/Sources/Fast/Design.swift#L548)

Yab shifts surrounding rows during drag, whereas the Arc recording shows stationary rows and a 2 pt insertion line. The Arc command bar dimensions recorded by Cra are 766 pt wide, with a 60 pt field and 50 pt rows. Yab caps the overlay at 750 pt standard or 540 pt compact, with field heights of 58 or 52 pt and row heights of 38 or 40 pt respectively. Cra recorded list-edge hairlines of 1 pt at 5% black in light mode and 7% white in dark mode. Yab uses masks and a foot gradient. Yab’s space dock can scroll when even the dot slots overflow. These source differences leave the Arc measurements intact. [Arc measurements](arc-ardia-observed.md), [Cra ledger](cra-ledger.md), [ColumnDrag.swift:831-854](https://github.com/yetanotherbrowser/yab/blob/5a9b57a94cdfbc9bb181b8bab26c1f0611ddf245/Sources/Fast/ColumnDrag.swift#L831-L854), [Omnibox.swift:67-91](https://github.com/yetanotherbrowser/yab/blob/5a9b57a94cdfbc9bb181b8bab26c1f0611ddf245/Sources/Fast/Omnibox.swift#L67-L91), [SpaceFoot.swift:198-225](https://github.com/yetanotherbrowser/yab/blob/5a9b57a94cdfbc9bb181b8bab26c1f0611ddf245/Sources/Fast/SpaceFoot.swift#L198-L225)
